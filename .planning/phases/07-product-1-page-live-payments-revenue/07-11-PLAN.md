<objective>
Wire the actual business logic into the `transaction.completed` branch of the Plan 08 webhook: send the buyer a purchase confirmation email (PAY-05), send the owner a new-order notification email (PAY-06), segment the buyer's Resend contact as `automate-buyer` (PROD-12), and fire the server-side Purchase analytics event (TRACK-03) — all server-side, per RESEARCH.md's explicit "never send transactional emails from client-side" rule (Pitfall 4) and its recommendation that the Purchase event fire from the webhook for reliability rather than a client callback.

Purpose: This is the last piece of the payment loop — without it, purchases complete in Paddle but nobody (buyer or owner) is notified, no DFY build can start without polling the dashboard, and the funnel dashboard never shows a completed purchase.
Output: Two React Email templates + `email.ts` helpers + the webhook route's `transaction.completed` branch fully wired (emails, segmentation, and analytics).
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| T-07-17 | Information Disclosure | Buyer email/amount logged to console on failure | mitigate | Log messages include the transaction ID and error message but never the full buyer email in plaintext console output beyond what's necessary for debugging (first-letter + domain masking is out of scope for a solo-operator server log, but no PII is sent to any third-party logging service in this phase) |
| T-07-18 | Repudiation | Duplicate confirmation/owner emails on webhook retry | accept | Same accepted-risk class as T-07-12 (no DB-backed idempotency this phase) — a retried webhook may send a second confirmation email to the same buyer; low severity, no financial impact |
