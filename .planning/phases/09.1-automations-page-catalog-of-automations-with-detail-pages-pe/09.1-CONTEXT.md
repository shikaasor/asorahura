<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Catalog Scope — Five Offerings (LOCKED)

1. **Instagram comment-to-DM** — "the front door." Auto-DMs Reel commenters with a link. Deliberately the cheapest, fastest-demoing product; functions as a qualification filter, not the main revenue driver. Already live as the Phase 7 `/automate` page — this becomes offering #1 in the catalog, not rebuilt from scratch.
   - **Live pricing (verified in `src/lib/checkout.ts` — use these exact figures, do not use any other number for this offering):** Done For You (DFY) $500 one-time; Done With You (DWY) $800 one-time; Care Plan (optional add-on) $9.99/mo; Build Map (free, self-host) $0.

2. **Email triage on Telegram** — "the flagship." Triages 20–30 emails/day via a Telegram bot interface (no new app for the client to learn). Recurring revenue driver — proven internally in "Kidaya."
   - Price: $1,500 setup + $250/mo.
   - **Known constraint (do not solve in this phase, but do not misrepresent in copy):** Gmail restricted-scopes / Google verification is required to serve multiple clients from one app (per-client OAuth or workspace-level install). This is an operational/backend gate, not a page-copy blocker — the detail page should not overpromise instant multi-client onboarding.

3. **Writing constitution + content** — "the highest margin." Two-part offer: (a) a one-off "writing constitution" deliverable (a document capturing the client's voice — already built internally, 8 modules, in Notion), sold as the paid artifact itself, not "we'll write your posts"; (b) an ongoing content-generation subscription that runs on top of the constitution.
   - Price: Constitution build $600 one-off. Content subscription $400/mo.
   - Copy framing requirement: sell the constitution as the differentiated deliverable — avoid framing this as a generic "we post for you" service (that framing invites revision cycles and undersells the differentiation).

4. **Rate-aware invoice and quote** — "the differentiator." Helps small businesses (e.g. Nigerian construction) quote jobs at current market rates instead of undercutting from ignorance.
   - Price: $1,000 setup + $150/mo.
   - **Scope constraint for copy/positioning (do not misrepresent capability):** this is NOT free-text AI price research (that hallucinates and is unsafe — a wrong number costs the client a contract). The real product is: client's own rate card + comparable jobs the client uploads + markup/margin logic + a research layer that cites sources and flags low confidence. AI does structure and markup discipline; it does not invent prices. The detail page copy must reflect this scoped, source-cited framing, not "AI knows market rates."

5. **Client onboarding agent** — "the expansion play." Watches Slack/Drive/Zoom and auto-provisions a welcome pack, channel, and folder for a new client in ~20 seconds. Best live demo of the five.
   - Price: $2,000 setup.
   - Positioning: targets agencies/consultancies (highest ticket, not the small-business self-serve buyer of offerings 1–4). Wide integration surface — position as available but understood to be the most complex to onboard.

### Resolved During UI-SPEC + Research (LOCKED)

- **Routing:** `/automate` becomes the catalog page. Offering #1 (Instagram) moves to `/automate/instagram`. Offerings 2–5 live at `/automate/[slug]`.
- **Redirect:** Old `/automate` links (Reel/marketing, already live) redirect to `/automate/instagram` — preserves existing traffic behavior; the catalog does not silently absorb Product #1's inbound links.
- **Success page:** Offering #1's existing success page moves from `/automate/success` to `/automate/instagram/success` (full consistency with the new per-offering route structure, not just a link patch).
- **Checkout strategy:** Offering #1 keeps its live Paddle checkout (DFY $500 / DWY $800 / Care Plan $9.99/mo). Offerings 2–5 ship with a waitlist/contact CTA this phase — Paddle checkout for them is explicitly deferred, not built now.
- **Waitlist CTA target:** Offerings 2–5's waitlist/contact CTA points at the existing `/engage` intake page — no new form/page is built for this in Phase 9.1.
- **Copy depth:** Offerings 2–5 ship with full real detail-page copy this phase, written from the descriptions/positioning already captured above — not placeholder/lorem-ipsum content.
