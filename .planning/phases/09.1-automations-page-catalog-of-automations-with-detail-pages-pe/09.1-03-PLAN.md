This plan resolves the conflict by honoring "/automate becomes the catalog page" as primary (matches the ROADMAP phase goal and 100% of UI-SPEC's catalog build-out) and does NOT add a blanket redirect on the bare `/automate` path. Reel-driven visitors landing on the catalog reach the Instagram offering one click away via the first card ("View Offering"). This resolution is surfaced to the user in the planner's final output for confirmation — it is not silently decided.
